# HR-Analytics-Dashboard
Project Overview: Enterprise Business Intelligence & Performance Dashboard

Problem Statement (What Was Resolved)

Eliminated fragmented, manual reporting workflows across disconnected business units.

Resolved data latency and calculation discrepancies by centralizing performance metrics into a single source of truth.

Replaced static tabular reports with interactive, executive-ready visualizations for rapid root-cause analysis and operational decision-making.

Tools & Technologies (What Was Used)

Core Platform: Microsoft Power BI Desktop & Power BI Service

Data Modeling & Calculations: DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for calculated measures, time intelligence, and KPI aggregations

ETL & Data Transformation: Power Query (M Language) for data cleansing, schema normalization, and automated pipelines

Data Modeling Architecture: Star Schema configuration with one-to-many relationships and dedicated dimension/fact tables

Input Data

Transactional Records: Historical business operations data, transactional sales/orders, revenue, and cost metrics.

Dimension Tables: Master dimension records including Calendar/Date tables, Region/Branch hierarchy, Customer/Account classifications, and Product/Service categories.

Target & Budget Sets: Monthly/quarterly operational and revenue baseline targets for variance tracking.

Output & Deliverables (What You Get)

Executive Summary Dashboard: High-level KPI scorecards tracking actuals vs. targets, YoY growth, and profit/margin performance.

Interactive Trend Analysis: Multi-tiered date hierarchies enabling instant drill-down from annual to monthly/daily transactional levels.

Dynamic Filtering & Slicers: Cross-filtering by regional distribution, category, operational status, and custom date ranges.

Variance & Outlier Detection: Automated visual flags highlighting underperforming business units and operational bottlenecks.
